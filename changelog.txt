Version: 0.5.2
Date: 2026-10-22
  Features:
    - New startup setting "Where can K2 things be made": Everywhere (nothing is restricted to K2), Mixed (default, same as before) or Mixed + everything with Imersite (every recipe that uses Imersite can only be made on K2).
    - The separate "Restrict Quarry", "Restrict Superior logistics" and "Restrict Advanced tech card" settings were removed. They are now part of the Mixed option.
    - New startup settings to generate extra resources on the K2 planet: water lakes, iron ore, copper ore, coal, stone, crude oil and uranium ore. All are disabled by default.
  Graphics:
    - Space platforms above K2 now show K2's own violet surface with stormy clouds instead of Nauvis.
  Bugfixes:
    - Old Fulgora-based generation: removed all Fulgora ruins, ruin tiles, scrap and plants. Only the natural Fulgora ground and rocks remain.

Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-20
  Info:
    - Factorio 2.0 version. From now on the 2.0 and 2.1 versions are released together (0.5.0 for 2.0, 0.5.1 for 2.1).
  Features:
    - Space platforms parked above K2 can now collect asteroids (a mix between Nauvis and Fulgora). With the Imersite-Asteroids mod, Imersite asteroids appear there too.
    - Added violet rocks, boulders, sand patches and dunes to the K2 planet (violet desert mode). Uses Alien Biomes graphics when available. The amount follows the "Rocks" map generation setting.
    - The violet boulders also drop some raw Imersite next to the stone when mined (1-3, 2-5 or 5-10 depending on size).
    - Added cargo pod and platform landing/departure animations, clouds and ambient wind and thunder sounds to the K2 planet.
  Graphics:
    - Added a proper 64px planet icon (with mipmaps) and a 256px technology icon.
    - Each violet tile now has its own map color, so the minimap is no longer a flat purple area.
    - Mineral water filtration recipe now has its own layered icon.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a progression softlock when "Allow Rare metals on Nauvis" is disabled: Rare metal processing could only be researched by mining rare metal ore. It is now a normal research in that case.
    - Imersite asteroids (Imersite-Asteroids mod) now really spawn with double chance on the K2 routes.
  Balance:
    - Factorio 2.0: Lowered the K2 planet's ground-level solar power from 200% to 150%, the same as in the 2.1 version.
  Locale:
    - Added the missing Nauvis - K2 route name and the mineral water filtration description.
    - Fixed and clarified several setting descriptions (English and Hungarian).
    - Removed leftover Age of Production compatibility entries and icons.
