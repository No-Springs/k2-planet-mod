# Krastorio 2 Planet — temporary unofficial copy

**Krastorio 2 Planet is the work of SargaPulcsis. All credit for the original mod belongs to him.**

- [SargaPulcsis and his mods](https://mods.factorio.com/user/SargaPulcsis)
- [Original Krastorio 2 Planet mod](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/k2-planet-mod)

The original mod is listed under the MIT license on the Factorio Mod Portal.

This temporary, unofficial repository exists only to submit proposed bug fixes to SargaPulcsis. It contains a copy of his version 0.5.3 and two proposed fixes for planets on pre-existing game saves. It is not an official release, a replacement for his mod, or a claim of authorship of his work. Each fix can be reviewed separately.

The mod files on `main` are unchanged from the original download. Each proposed fix is on its own branch.
